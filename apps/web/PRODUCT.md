# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers operating one or more coding-agent sessions across local, desktop,
web, and mobile environments. They use shuv2code for sustained, high-attention
work and need the interface to recede behind session navigation, review, and
control.

## Product Purpose

shuv2code is the local-first control plane for coding agents. It brings Codex,
Claude, Cursor, and OpenCode sessions into one workspace while keeping local
and self-hosted operation the default.

## Brand Personality

Confident, restrained, and mischievous. The devil-terminal mark supplies the
personality; the working interface should remain calm and precise.

## Anti-references

- Bright, saturated color spread across routine controls or large decorative
  surfaces.
- Generic neon "developer tool" gradients and glowing cyberpunk chrome.
- SaaS-dashboard ornament, glass cards, or visual effects that compete with
  active work.
- Upstream changes obscured by unnecessary structural or component churn.

## Design Principles

- Let the tool disappear into the task.
- Reserve brand personality for a few recognizable moments.
- Use the shared brick family for interaction and identity, reserve semantic
  red for genuine danger, and let midnight navy or warm paper carry the rest.
- Preserve familiar product affordances and readable state hierarchy.
- Keep visual changes token-driven and sync-friendly with upstream.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Do not rely on color alone for
status, preserve visible keyboard focus, support reduced motion, and keep
semantic error colors distinct without making them decorative.
